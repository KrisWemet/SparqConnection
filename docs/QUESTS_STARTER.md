# Starter Quests (Loose Structures for Personalization)

Each day has: **story\_seed • daily\_question • micro\_action • journal • reflection • tags\[]**.

## Quest 1: Emotional Safety Foundations (7 days)

**Intent**: Slow down reactivity; build predictability and warmth.

* **Day 1**
  story: A small moment where one partner took a breath before replying.
  dq: "What helps you feel safe to share when something's awkward?"
  action: "Before answering, inhale 4, exhale 6 once today."
  journal: "One situation where a pause helped (or could have)."
  reflection: "Name one cue that says 'it's safe here.'"
  tags: \[`light`,`mindfulness`]
* **Day 2**
  story: Reassurance after a misread tone.
  dq: "Which kinds of check‑ins feel supportive vs. smothering?"
  action: "Send a short check‑in with choice (e.g., 'text/voice/no reply needed')."
  journal: "How did offering choice land for us?"
  reflection: "One phrasing I liked hearing today."
  tags: \[`avoidant`,`nvc`,`parents`]
* **Day 3**
  story: Owning a small mistake quickly.
  dq: "What makes apologies feel sincere to you?"
  action: "Use a 3‑part repair: name impact • own it • offer next time."
  journal: "A time I appreciated a quick repair."
  reflection: "One tiny 'next time' I can try."
  tags: \[`repair`,`gottman`]
* **Day 4**
  story: Turning down the volume on a tense topic.
  dq: "When should we take a 10‑minute pause vs. keep going?"
  action: "Suggest a timed pause once today, then return kindly."
  journal: "Pause helped/hurt because…"
  reflection: "A better re‑entry line for us is…"
  tags: \[`mindfulness`,`deep`]
* **Day 5**
  story: Appreciating effort, not outcome.
  dq: "What kind of effort feels seen for you?"
  action: "Spot one effort out loud within 24 hours."
  journal: "What I noticed them trying."
  reflection: "How did naming effort shift the mood?"
  tags: \[`words`,`nvc`,`light`]
* **Day 6**
  story: Predictable check‑ins reduce anxiety.
  dq: "What rhythm of updates lowers stress (daily? when late?)"
  action: "Agree on one predictable update for this week."
  journal: "What rhythm felt good today?"
  reflection: "One boundary that made things easier."
  tags: \[`anxious`,`parents`,`roommates`]
* **Day 7**
  story: Rituals that say "we're ok."
  dq: "What tiny ritual could anchor us this week?"
  action: "Start one 2‑minute ritual (eg tea & 1 question)."
  journal: "How did that ritual feel?"
  reflection: "One tweak to make it stick."
  tags: \[`light`,`mindfulness`]

## Quest 2: Appreciation & Bids (7 days)

**Intent**: See and respond to positive bids; express in top Love Languages.

* Day 1:
  story: A small bid ("look at this meme!") lands.
  dq: "What do your bids usually look like?"
  action: "Turn toward one bid today (even briefly)."
  journal: "A bid I might miss sometimes is…"
  reflection: "How did turning toward feel?"
  tags: \[`light`,`gottman`]
* Day 2:
  story: Noticing acts vs. words.
  dq: "Which appreciations land best (acts/words/time/gifts/touch)?"
  action: "Give one appreciation in their top LL."
  journal: "What I appreciated today."
  reflection: "Which template felt natural?"
  tags: \[`acts`,`words`,`time`]
* Day 3:
  story: Specific over generic.
  dq: "What makes a compliment feel real?"
  action: "Use a 2‑part appreciation: specific behavior → impact."
  journal: "I noticed you \_\_\_ and it helped \_\_\_."
  reflection: "One area I want to notice more."
  tags: \[`words`,`nvc`]
* Day 4:
  story: Bid repair after a miss.
  dq: "When a bid is missed, what repair helps?"
  action: "Name the miss + small make‑good."
  journal: "A bid I want to repair."
  reflection: "What made repair easier?"
  tags: \[`repair`,`gottman`]
* Day 5:
  story: Quiet bids from avoidant partner.
  dq: "What are your subtle bids?"
  action: "Check in with choice (reply optional)."
  journal: "Which subtle cue I noticed."
  reflection: "How choice changed the feel."
  tags: \[`avoidant`,`light`]
* Day 6:
  story: Public vs private appreciation.
  dq: "Where do appreciations feel best (text, voice, in person)?"
  action: "Send one in preferred channel."
  journal: "How did the channel impact it?"
  reflection: "One channel to avoid."
  tags: \[`words`,`time`]
* Day 7:
  story: Appreciation ritual.
  dq: "What 1‑minute ritual keeps appreciation alive?"
  action: "Start a nightly one‑liner ritual."
  journal: "The line I shared."
  reflection: "How to keep it fresh."
  tags: \[`light`,`ritual`]

## Quest 3: Repair Basics (No‑Trauma) (7 days)

**Intent**: Gentle repairs for everyday friction.

* Day 1: dq "What makes 'I'm sorry' feel real?" action "3‑part repair." tags \[`repair`,`nvc`]
* Day 2: dq "How should we signal 'I need a pause'?" action "Create a pause cue + re‑entry line." tags \[`mindfulness`]
* Day 3: dq "What's a small do‑over from this week?" action "Ask for a redo, kindly." tags \[`light`,`repair`]
* Day 4: dq "How do you prefer to be approached about hard stuff?" action "Ask consent before feedback." tags \[`consent`,`nvc`]
* Day 5: dq "What boundary reduces repeat friction?" action "Set one boundary kindly + reason." tags \[`boundaries`]
* Day 6: dq "What helps you believe change is coming?" action "Name one 'next time I'll…'" tags \[`repair`]
* Day 7: dq "How can we celebrate repairs?" action "Acknowledge a repair with gratitude." tags \[`words`,`light`]

## Quest 4: Playful Resets (7 days)

**Intent**: Reduce seriousness; add lightness and shared fun.

* Day 1: dq "What silly thing reliably makes you smile?" action "Send one playful thing today." tags \[`light`,`play`]
* Day 2: dq "What kind of game sounds fun for 3 minutes?" action "Try 'You or Me?' round." tags \[`play`]
* Day 3: dq "Favorite mini date at home?" action "Plan a 20‑min mini date." tags \[`time`,`parents`]
* Day 4: dq "Music that shifts your mood?" action "Share one song; listen together." tags \[`light`]
* Day 5: dq "A tradition we'd enjoy weekly?" action "Pilot it once." tags \[`ritual`,`light`]
* Day 6: dq "How do you like to be invited to play?" action "Invite with choice." tags \[`avoidant`,`play`]
* Day 7: dq "What surprise would feel sweet, not stressful?" action "Do a tiny surprise (opt‑out friendly)." tags \[`consent`,`light`]
