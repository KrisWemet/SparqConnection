# Privacy Policy (MVP)

**Summary:** We collect the minimum needed to run Sparq. Journals are end‑to‑end encrypted by default. We don't sell personal data. You can export or delete your data at any time (30‑day grace for recovery).

**Data we process:** account info, pairing info, daily plan metadata, encrypted journals, answers and actions, notification tokens, subscription status, analytics events (aggregated), error logs (non‑PII where possible).

**Security:** RLS on all tables, encryption in transit; journals are client‑encrypted. Access to production data is restricted and audited.

**Your choices:** sexuality content opt‑in; analytics opt‑out; marketing opt‑in.

**Deletion:** soft delete for 30 days, then purge. See `DATA_LIFECYCLE.md`.

**Age:** 18+ only.
