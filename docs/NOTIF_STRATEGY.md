# Notification Strategy

* **Provider**: OneSignal (PWA). Consider Web Push native fallback in v1.
* **Caps**: 1 ritual/day, 1 identity spot/day, ≤3 Play invites/week.
* **Snooze**: 1h/3h/tonight. **Jitter**: ±5m reminders; ±15m bulk.
* **Copy**: celebratory, no spoilers. Examples in `NOTIFICATIONS_copy_and_rules_v0.3.md`.
