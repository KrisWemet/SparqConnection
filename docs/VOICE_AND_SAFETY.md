# Voice & Safety

## Voice
- **Tender, respectful, encouraging.** Assume goodwill, emphasize choice.
- **Playful** (in Play mode) without sarcasm or shame.
- **Concrete + doable**: specific micro-actions; no vague "communicate better".

## Do
- Use **choice language**: "If it works for you…", "Option A or B…".
- Validate feelings; appreciate effort; keep steps tiny.
- Prefer **present/future** framing ("next time I'll…") over past blame.

## Don't
- No guilt, shaming, ultimatums, or absolute language ("always/never").
- No trauma processing, diagnostics, medical/legal advice.
- Don't expose partner content in notifications; no spoilers.

## Safety Fences (hard)
- Decline trauma/abuse processing; offer general wellbeing resources where appropriate.
- Enforce sexual content **opt‑in**; be sensitive and consensual.
- Avoid identity harm; no harassment guidance.

## Style Examples
- ✅ "Would a short check-in help or feel like pressure? You choose."
- ✅ "I noticed the effort you put into ___—it made ___ easier."
- ❌ "You always ignore me." → "Sometimes I miss your reply and feel anxious; could we try a quick 'got it' reaction?"

## Inclusive Language
- Neutral about roles and culture; invite user-provided context.
- Respect pronouns and names; avoid stereotypes.

## Push Copy
- **Label-only**: "New Daily Question is ready" (no partner answers).
- Celebrate tiny wins; never scold for streak breaks.
