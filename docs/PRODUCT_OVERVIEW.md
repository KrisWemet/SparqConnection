# Sparq Connection — Product Overview (Canonical)

## What Sparq Is
A daily, 5–8 minute ritual (plus playful extras) that helps couples build a kinder, more connected relationship. It blends light prompts, tiny actions, reflection, and appreciations—personalized by attachment style, love languages, context, and boundaries.

## Who It's For
- **Busy couples** wanting a low-lift, high-trust daily touchpoint.
- **Growth-ready partners** who like structured guidance.
- **Reluctant/avoidant partners** who prefer autonomy and "opt-in" vs. pressure.

## Core Value
- A **gentle daily ritual** that feels doable and positive.
- **Personalization** that respects preferences (e.g., avoidant + Acts of Service).
- **Playful engagement** to keep momentum without guilt or lectures.

## Pillars
1) **Today**: Identity → Center‑Me → Daily Question → Micro‑Action → Journal → Appreciation → Reflection.  
2) **Play**: Asynchronous rounds (e.g., "You or Me?", trivia, prompts) to lower resistance.  
3) **Plans**: Light planner for date ideas and upcoming mini-rituals.  
4) **Notes**: Partner-visible replies (scoped to items), not global chat.

## Tiers
- **Free**: Daily ritual (one per day), basic Play deck, limited saves, essential reminders.
- **Premium**: Full quest library, Planner, advanced Play, custom identities, AI tips.
- **Ultimate** (optional later): Live workshops/coaching drops, advanced analytics, seasonal libraries.

## Success Metrics
- D1 & D7 activation; weekly ritual completion; streaks; partner cross-participation rate; NPS.
- Qualitative: "felt seen", "less friction", "more warmth".

## Guardrails
- Not therapy; no trauma processing or diagnoses.
- E2EE for journals by default; RLS everywhere; label-only notifications.

## Differentiators
- All-in-one: ritual + play + actionable coaching, not just Q&A.
- Deep personalization with **ethical** tone and transparent pricing.
